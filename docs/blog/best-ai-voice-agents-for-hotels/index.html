Best AI voice agents for hotels in 2026: 10 options compared

Ten AI phone agents built for hotels, compared on the same six questions: do they book on the call, take the deposit, connect to your PMS, hand off cleanly, who are they for, and what do they cost.

Quick verdict

The best AI voice agent for a hotel is one that finishes the job on the call: it checks live availability, books the stay into your PMS, takes the deposit and hands anything tricky to a person. Of the ten we compared, Dextr's Daisy is the strongest fit for independent and franchised hotels where the phone still books rooms, and for campgrounds, RV parks and rental portfolios. Canary AI Voice suits hotels already on Canary's guest platform, Conduit suits operators who want calls and texts on one agent with a published price, and PolyAI suits large groups and casino resorts.

Disclosure: this guide is published by Dextr, which makes Daisy, one of the voice agents below. We compared every product on the same six questions, using each company's own website and press releases, and we link to those pages so you can check the facts yourself. Everything was checked on 14 October 2026.

Key takeaways

  • Ask one question first: can the agent book a room during the call, or does it only take a message? Most hotel-built agents now book on the call.
  • Deposits are where they differ most. Daisy, Akia, PolyAI and EHVA say they take payment during the call. Others mention payments or deposits without saying how they work on a call.
  • Three of the ten show a price up front: Conduit, Sadie and EHVA. Everyone else, Dextr included, quotes on request.
  • Few hotel voice agents talk about campgrounds and RV parks. Dextr markets its agents as purpose-built for both.
  • Before you sign, make real test calls: a booking, a change, an after-hours question and a request for a person.

How we compared them

We asked the same six questions of every product, in the order a hotel buyer usually does:

  1. Does it book the room during the call, or only take a message?
  2. Can it take the deposit or payment during the call?
  3. Which booking systems (PMS) does it name?
  4. What happens when it can't help, and what does your team see?
  5. What kind of property is it built for?
  6. Is the price published?

We read each company's own product pages, pricing pages and press releases on 14 October 2026, and linked the key pages in each entry. Where a fact comes only from the vendor, we say so. We left out tools whose AI only chats, and tools that no longer sell a voice product; they're listed further down so you know why. We did not place test calls to every vendor for this guide, so use the test calls in the demo section to compare the ones on your shortlist.

Key takeaway

Treat every number below as the vendor's own claim until you hear the agent handle your calls.

The 10 best AI voice agents for hotels

1. Dextr (Daisy)

Best for: independent and franchised hotels where the phone still books rooms, plus campgrounds, RV parks and vacation-rental portfolios.

Daisy is Dextr's AI voice agent. It answers your main line 24/7, checks availability, quotes the rate, books, changes and cancels reservations, and takes the deposit during the call; payment links and follow-ups go to the guest by text. It offers upsells such as room upgrades, early check-in and late checkout, and passes anything outside its knowledge base to your team with the caller's details attached. In some of Dextr's sample calls and case studies the agent uses a male voice named Danny.

  • Booking systems: OPERA Cloud, Mews, Cloudbeds, Stayntouch, ResNexus, Hostaway, Guesty and Apaleo, plus more than 40 vacation-rental systems through one connection (integrations).
  • Setup: live in about seven days. Your phone number, phones and PMS stay as they are.
  • Payments and security: Dextr never stores raw card numbers; tokenization is handled by your PCI-compliant payment processor. Dextr states it has completed a SOC 2 Type II audit (responsible AI).
  • Keeping it current: owners update policies, rates and discount codes in plain English, and every call is scored for quality.
  • Results: at one family-run RV park, revenue grew 34% year on year and the voice agent took more than $9,474 by phone across 39 bookings. At two properties of a 50-plus property hotel chain, the AI added about $80,000 in revenue per property in its first two months. Each is one customer's result; the RV park's name was changed and the hotel chain isn't named.
  • Pricing: usage-based and not published; Dextr offers a pilot measured against the property's own trend.
  • Not the right fit if: you need a public price list before speaking to anyone.

2. Canary AI Voice

Best for: hotels and brands already using Canary's guest platform for mobile check-in, upsells and guest messaging.

Canary AI Voice launched in February 2025. It answers every call 24/7, answers guest questions, books and changes stays, promotes upsells and routes calls to the right team, while keeping your existing phone system and call-center software. Canary says its products are used by more than 20,000 hoteliers.

  • Booking systems: the voice page refers to PMS, CRS, CRM, booking engine and payment systems without naming specific ones. Canary's integrations page lists PMSs such as Opera, RoomMaster, StayNTouch, Mews and Cloudbeds, but doesn't say which of them work with AI Voice.
  • Payments: the voice page doesn't say it takes payment during the call; Canary sells payment tools as separate products.
  • Worth knowing: Canary says AI Voice won the 2026 HotelTechAwards category for voice and call-center software.
  • Pricing: quote only.
  • Worth asking: Canary's pricing page doesn't list AI Voice in any bundle, so ask whether it can be bought and priced on its own.

3. Conduit

Best for: hotels and rental operators who want one AI agent for calls and texts, with a published price.

Conduit's voice agent answers calls 24/7, matches the caller to their reservation in the PMS, answers questions and escalates with context. Since June 2026, Conduit says it can also take a booking in the PMS, change a reservation and reset a door code during the call.

  • Booking systems: Opera Cloud, Mews, Cloudbeds, Apaleo, RMS Cloud and StayNTouch, plus rental systems such as Guesty and Hostaway (integrations).
  • Pricing: $899 a month on the Startup plan for up to 25 units; larger portfolios start at $18 per unit a month on a credit-based plan that includes voice (pricing).
  • Payments: Conduit's blog says its agent can charge fees when guests call, for example for a late checkout or early check-in. We found no Conduit page that describes taking a booking deposit on the call.
  • Languages: detects the caller's language automatically; Conduit claims 140+ languages across voice and text, though its hotel page gives no number.
  • Check first: ask to see a deposit taken on a live call.

4. PolyAI

Best for: large hotel groups, casino resorts and central reservation teams.

PolyAI builds enterprise voice agents and has a dedicated hotel offering. Its agents take room reservations, start housekeeping requests, handle billing questions and route calls to staff, and PolyAI says it can scale across hundreds of sites and take payment over the phone. In its Golden Nugget case study, PolyAI reports more than 300 completed reservations a week within two months of launch.

  • Booking systems: "most property management systems", with none named on the hotel page.
  • Pricing: charged per minute; PolyAI's pricing page doesn't give the rate for its voice agent.
  • Worth knowing: Travel Outlook's Annette agent runs on PolyAI; Travel Outlook announced an upgraded version in February 2026.
  • Not the right fit if: you run a single independent property; both hotel case studies on PolyAI's hotel page are large casino resorts.

5. RateGain UNO VIVA

Best for: hotels and groups already using RateGain's distribution and central reservation tools.

UNO VIVA handles inbound reservation calls, quotes live rates and availability, books, changes bookings, upsells and escalates to staff, then sends confirmations to the guest and the PMS. RateGain says it works in more than 30 languages. It launched in April 2025.

  • Booking systems: "leading CRS and PMS systems, including RateGain's own suite"; no third-party PMS is named.
  • Payments: the page says VIVA is GDPR and PCI compliant but doesn't say it takes payment on the call.
  • Setup: RateGain says a typical go-live takes two to four weeks.
  • Pricing: demo request only.

6. KITT by The Hotels Network

Best for: hotels already using The Hotels Network's direct-booking tools, especially on OPERA Cloud.

KITT works by phone and on the hotel website. It handles booking inquiries and reservations 24/7, promotes offers, and sends the hotel a translated summary of each conversation. The Hotels Network's 2025 release says its OPERA Cloud connection can book reservations and process payments.

  • Booking systems: OPERA Cloud, through Oracle's integration platform (OHIP), per the 2025 release; the KITT page itself names no PMS.
  • Pricing: demo request only.
  • Not the right fit if: you need the agent to write bookings into a PMS other than OPERA Cloud. That's the only one The Hotels Network names for KITT, so ask whether it supports yours.

7. Akia AI Voice

Best for: hotels and vacation rentals that want calls and texts on one agent, with payment during the call.

Akia's voice agent answers on the first ring, recognizes the caller from the PMS, books rooms and answers rate and availability questions. Akia says it texts the guest a secure, PCI-compliant payment link during the call, and hands off to staff with a summary.

  • Booking systems: Mews, Cloudbeds, StayNTouch, Guesty and Hostaway by name; the page also shows logos for others, including Oracle Opera and Apaleo, and says Akia connects to "dozens" of PMSs.
  • Pricing: voice is in Akia's top tier, and every tier is "talk to sales".

8. Myma.ai

Best for: hotels on Opera Cloud, Mews, Cloudbeds or RMS Cloud.

Myma's voice agent says it answers in under two seconds. It handles availability, rate quotes, bookings, changes, in-stay requests and upsells, writes confirmed bookings back to the PMS and texts the guest a confirmation. Complex calls go to staff with a transcript. Myma's site also mentions holiday parks.

  • Booking systems: Oracle Opera Cloud, Mews, Cloudbeds and RMS Cloud.
  • Languages: its page gives both "over 50" and "10+", so ask which apply to you.
  • Pricing: custom quote.

9. Sadie

Best for: independent hotels on Opera, Mews, RMS, Cloudbeds and other PMSs.

Sadie answers calls 24/7, creates and manages room reservations in the PMS, and answers concierge questions such as check-in times, directions and local recommendations. It also serves restaurants, and it powers roommaster's Concierge agent.

  • Booking systems: Oracle Opera, Mews, RMS, Cloudbeds, Stayntouch, ResNexus, Innkeeper's Advantage, Yanolja and others (hotel integrations); the Cloudbeds connection was added in January 2026.
  • Pricing: published hotel plans at $199, $279 and $499 a month for up to 300, 600 and 1,200 minutes, with Enterprise on request. Each plan lists "secure deposits" (pricing).

10. EHVA

Best for: full-service hotels with heavy guest-room and in-room dining calls, especially those on Stayntouch.

EHVA answers the main line and guest-room calls, logs amenity and maintenance requests, approves late checkout, takes in-room dining orders through the POS and texts a secure payment link. Its April 2026 release with Stayntouch says it handles booking calls end to end for Stayntouch hotels.

  • Booking systems: Stayntouch is the only PMS it names; it says it works with most major PMS platforms and booking engines.
  • Results: EHVA says The Argonaut Hotel in San Francisco cut more than 1,200 front-desk calls a month.
  • Pricing: EHVA's homepage says about 9 cents a minute with no contract; its pricing page gives no hotel-specific price.

Key takeaway

If the phone books rooms at your property, shortlist agents that book and take the deposit on the same call, and that name your PMS. That narrows ten options to three or four very quickly.

Also worth knowing

These six are real hotel voice products, but each is newer, narrower or built differently from the ten above:

  • SendSquared: answers with PMS context and qualifies sales calls, then warm-transfers them; its hotel page says a human always closes the booking. It supports more than 30 languages.
  • Grevon Echo: books into the PMS and sells add-ons; it debuted at HITEC in June 2026, so it is very new.
  • Riviera: a Y Combinator startup (Winter 2025) covering calls, in-room dining orders and reservations.
  • Annette by Travel Outlook: runs on PolyAI and sits alongside Travel Outlook's human reservations team.
  • roommaster Concierge: the AI phone concierge sold by roommaster, a hotel PMS company, and powered by Sadie.
  • Aiello AI Call Agent: a general call agent from an Asia-based hotel AI company; it books hotel restaurant tables through TableCheck.

Build-it-yourself voice platforms

Retell, Bland and Synthflow sell platforms for building your own phone agent, and each has a hospitality page. None names a hotel PMS: on self-serve plans you or a developer build that connection and keep it working, and their enterprise plans add the vendor's own engineers.

Tools whose AI doesn't answer the hotel phone

Some voice-agent lists include HiJiffy and Asksuite, but none of these three has an AI that answers live phone calls:

  • HiJiffy: its voicebot answers voice notes guests send in WhatsApp and other chat apps.
  • Asksuite: its AI works in text channels; its phone system, sold only in Brazil, is for human agents.
  • Quicktext: now Quinta, a hotel-data company; its Velma assistant is chat-based.

What an AI voice agent costs a hotel

Most hotel voice agents don't publish a price. Among the ten above, three do:

  • Conduit: $899 a month for up to 25 units, and from $18 per unit a month, voice included, for larger portfolios.
  • Sadie: hotel plans at $199, $279 and $499 a month for up to 300, 600 and 1,200 minutes.
  • EHVA: about 9 cents a minute with no contract, according to its homepage.

PolyAI charges per minute without giving the rate on its pricing page. Retell and Bland list $0.07 to $0.31 per minute plus phone costs, and Synthflow's contracts start at $30,000 a year. Dextr's pricing is usage-based and depends on property size, workflows and usage volume.

When you compare quotes, ask:

  • Is it priced per room, per call, per minute or per property?
  • What counts as a call, and are after-hours calls priced differently?
  • Are setup, PMS connection and onboarding included?
  • Can you start with one property, or with nights and weekends only?

Key takeaway

Compare quotes against the bookings you miss today, not just the monthly fee. An agent that books and takes the deposit on the call earns its cost back on the calls nobody answers now.

Campgrounds, RV parks and vacation rentals

Most hotel voice agents are built around hotel booking systems. If you sell campsites or RV sites, ask whether the agent connects to your reservation system and understands site types, hookups, rig lengths and pet rules.

Dextr markets its agents as purpose-built for campgrounds, RV parks and glamping, connects to ResNexus and RMS Cloud, and reaches more than 40 vacation-rental systems through one connection. Myma's site mentions holiday parks, and Conduit, Akia and SendSquared all serve vacation-rental operators.

Key takeaway

For outdoor properties the booking system decides it. Confirm the agent can book a real site in your software before anything else.

Will guests know they're talking to an AI?

They often ask, and the safest answer is yes. Set the agent to say it's an AI when asked, and make it easy to reach a person at any point. Dextr's agents say they're AI when asked and always leave a route to your team. Rules on AI disclosure and call recording vary by state and province, so check yours before you go live.

When an AI voice agent isn't the right choice

  • Most of your calls are complex group or event sales. An agent can qualify them, but a person should close.
  • Your PMS has no connection to the agent. Without one, it can only take messages, which is what voicemail already does.
  • Nobody can review calls in the first few weeks. Every agent needs its answers checked and corrected early on.
  • Your brand promises a person on every call. Use AI for after-hours and overflow only.

Questions to ask in any demo

  1. Can you book a room on this call, into my PMS, while I watch?
  2. Can you take a deposit during the call, and how is the card handled?
  3. What happens when I ask for a person, and what does my team see?
  4. If I call after hours with a question you can't answer, what happens next?
  5. Which languages do you support, and can I test one of mine now?
  6. Can I change a policy myself, and how soon does the agent use it?
  7. What exactly will I pay each month, and what counts as a call?
  8. Can I hear recorded calls from a property like mine?

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI voice agent for hotels?

It depends on your property. For independent and franchised hotels where the phone books rooms, Dextr's Daisy books the stay, takes the deposit and hands off to staff on the same call. Canary AI Voice suits hotels already on Canary, Conduit suits operators who want calls and texts on one agent with a published price, and PolyAI suits large groups and casino resorts.

Can an AI voice agent book a hotel room over the phone?

Yes, if it connects to your PMS and is allowed to create reservations. Most of the ten agents in this guide say they book during the call. Ask to see a live booking land in your own PMS during the demo.

How much does an AI voice agent cost for a hotel?

Most vendors quote on request. Conduit publishes $899 a month for up to 25 units and from $18 per unit a month for larger portfolios, Sadie's hotel plans start at $199 a month for up to 300 minutes, and Retell and Bland charge $0.07 to $0.31 per minute plus phone costs. Dextr's pricing is usage-based and depends on property size, workflows and usage volume.

Will an AI voice agent replace my front desk?

No. It answers routine calls such as availability, rates, bookings and directions, so your team can focus on guests in front of them. Anything outside its knowledge goes to a person with the details already captured.

Does an AI voice agent work with my PMS?

Check the vendor's named list. Dextr names OPERA Cloud, Mews, Cloudbeds, Stayntouch, ResNexus, Hostaway, Guesty and Apaleo. Also ask whether the agent only reads your PMS or can write bookings into it.

Can an AI voice agent take card payments safely?

The common approach is to text the guest a secure payment link, so the card number is never read aloud. With Dextr, raw card numbers are never stored; tokenization is handled by your PCI-compliant payment processor. Ask every vendor how cards are handled and which PCI standard applies.

How long does it take to set up an AI voice agent?

Dextr goes live in about seven days, and your phone number, phones and PMS stay as they are. Other vendors range from days to weeks; RateGain, for example, says a typical VIVA go-live takes two to four weeks. Ask for a dated plan.

Do AI voice agents speak Spanish and other languages?

Most handle several languages, but the counts vary and some vendors give more than one figure. Test the languages your guests actually use during the demo.
