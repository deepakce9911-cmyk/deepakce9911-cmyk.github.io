# Dextr vs Canary AI Voice (2026): which AI should answer your hotel's phone?

**Quick verdict:** Dextr is a dedicated AI voice agent for hotels: its agent, Daisy, is built to answer the phone, book the stay and send the deposit link before the call ends. Canary AI Voice is a voice layer on Canary's wider guest platform, which also covers mobile check-in, digital keys, upsells and guest texting. Choose Dextr if the phone is your main booking channel, or if you run a campground, RV park or rental portfolio. Choose Canary if you want one platform for the whole guest journey, with voice included.

Dextr builds AI agents for hospitality. Its voice agent, Daisy, answers a property's main line day and night, checks availability, quotes the rate, books the stay and sends a payment link for the deposit before the call ends. Dextr also sells agents for guest messaging, housekeeping and maintenance, marketing and group sales.

Canary Technologies sells a web-based guest management platform used by more than 20,000 hotels: mobile check-in, mobile keys, upsells, guest texting, tipping and payments. In February 2025 it added an AI phone agent, Canary AI Voice, which Wyndham has rolled out to its franchisees after a pilot at more than 700 hotels.

The two come from different starting points. Canary added a phone agent to a guest platform. Dextr built its product around the phone call and the hotel work that follows it.

> **Key takeaways**
> - **Bookings:** both take and change bookings in your booking software. Dextr's Daisy also cancels them.
> - **Deposits:** Dextr sends the deposit payment link during the call. Canary handles payment through separate products, such as payment links that hotel staff send by text or email.
> - **Handoffs:** both pass hard calls to a person with the caller's details captured.
> - **Beyond the phone:** Canary offers the broader guest-facing suite, including mobile keys and a self-service kiosk. Dextr adds agents for housekeeping, maintenance and staff scheduling.
> - **Support and scale:** Canary includes 24/7 support and runs at more than 20,000 hotels. Dextr assigns each client a dedicated engineer and an AI strategist.

> **Key takeaway:** on the core phone job the two are close. The differences sit around the call: how the deposit is collected, what else each platform covers, and how support works.

## Taking a booking by phone

Picture a 90-room hotel at 9:40 pm on a Saturday. A guest calls about two rooms for next Friday, with a dog.

With Dextr, Daisy checks availability in your booking software, quotes the rate, answers the pet question from your own policy and books the rooms. Before the call ends she sends a payment link for the deposit. Daisy can also change or cancel an existing booking on the same call.

With Canary, AI Voice also answers around the clock, books the stay and can change an existing booking, and the booking appears in your booking software. Canary says its agent decides when to offer upsells and cross-sells during the call.

Both offer extras on the call. Dextr offers extras such as late checkout, firewood and golf carts. At two properties of a 50-plus property hotel chain, bookings taken by Dextr's AI carried [15 to 17% higher average room rates](https://www.dextr.ai/case-studies/reservation-contact-centers/).

> **Key takeaway:** both can take the booking end to end. Daisy also handles cancellations; Canary's AI chooses when to offer upsells.

## Deposits and payments

A deposit is the money a guest pays upfront to hold a room. Guests who have paid one are less likely to cancel or not show up, so when and how it is collected matters.

Dextr asks for it inside the call. Its AI sends the caller a payment link before the call ends, so the guest can pay straight away. One family-run RV park took more than $9,474 by phone this way across 39 bookings. Dextr never stores raw card numbers; your payment processor, such as Stripe, secures them.

Canary collects payment with dedicated products. Hotel staff create a payment link and send it by text or email, and Canary can generate links automatically from the booking's rate and status. Canary also offers an online card form and mobile check-in, where the guest adds a card before arrival.

> **Key takeaway:** Dextr ties the deposit to the phone call. Canary runs payments as their own products that work across every channel, not just the phone.

## When the AI can't answer

Every AI phone agent meets questions it should not answer alone, such as a wedding block, a refund or an upset guest. What matters is how it hands over.

Dextr's rule is to hand over rather than guess. Daisy answers what she is sure of, answers borderline questions but flags them for review, and passes anything outside her knowledge to your team. At one RV park, Dextr's AI handed 415 of 1,419 calls and texts to the owners, with the guest's name, dates and request already captured.

Canary says AI Voice callers are "answered instantly or routed to the right person." One of its hotels says transferred calls arrive with the guest's details already captured, so staff know who is calling and why.

> **Key takeaway:** both pass hard calls to a person with the context attached, so staff never start from zero.

## Keeping answers up to date

Rates, policies and road closures change every week, and the AI has to keep up.

With Dextr, you describe the change in plain English, the way you would brief a new team member. At one RV park, the owners told Dextr that dogs over 50 lbs were now allowed, and the voice agent used the new rule on future calls. Every call is also scored for tone, guest frustration and outcome, and calls that go wrong are flagged for review.

With Canary, the AI works from a knowledge base, a store of facts about your hotel, which grows as it talks to guests. Staff can rate each AI reply as good or bad, which Canary calls a thumbs up or thumbs down, and the AI learns from those ratings. Staff can also watch conversations live and switch the AI off.

> **Key takeaway:** Dextr is updated by instruction and checked call by call. Canary learns from conversations and staff ratings.

## Beyond the phone

A booking creates more work: a door code, a cleaning task, a pre-arrival message, a late checkout request. The two companies cover this differently.

Canary's strength is the guest-facing journey. Its platform includes mobile check-in, tablet registration, a self-service kiosk, mobile keys, upsells, guest texting, room-service ordering, tipping and checkout, plus tools for group sales, payments and contracts. For housekeeping and maintenance, its AI turns guest messages into tickets that staff approve and send to partner tools such as Quore and HotSOS.

Dextr's strength is the operations side. Alfred answers guest texts across SMS, WhatsApp and the booking sites, and handles check-in and tipping. Manny builds the housekeeping board, assigns maintenance jobs and schedules staff from occupancy. Cosmo writes marketing emails for your approval, a group sales agent works through group and event emails, and DOSS lets staff run operations by chatting in plain language.

> **Key takeaway:** choose Canary for the widest guest-facing toolkit. Choose Dextr if you also want AI to run housekeeping, maintenance and staff scheduling.

## Booking-system connections

Both connect to the booking software hotels already use, which is what lets the AI read availability and write reservations.

Dextr has more than 50 live connections to hotel and rental booking software, including OPERA Cloud, Cloudbeds, Mews, Stayntouch, ResNexus and Hostaway, and builds new connections on request.

Canary connects to about 30 hotel booking systems, including Oracle, Cloudbeds, Mews, Stayntouch, Maestro, Infor and Agilysys, plus central reservation systems used by chains such as SynXis and Amadeus. It says it launches 5 to 10 new integrations a quarter.

> **Key takeaway:** both cover the major hotel systems. Dextr adds dozens of vacation-rental systems; Canary adds the central reservation systems chains rely on.

## Campgrounds, RV parks and rentals

Outdoor stays have their own questions: site types, hookups, pets, firewood, gate codes. Owners are often outside working when the phone rings.

Dextr has dedicated RV park and campground pages and two outdoor case studies, and connects to ResNexus and RMS Cloud, two of the major campground systems. Through RemoteLock, its agents can resend a guest's access code by call or text.

Canary has a vacation-rental page that includes AI Voice and a customer story from a lakeside resort near Yosemite. Its site has no campground or RV park pages.

> **Key takeaway:** for hotels, both are options. For campgrounds and RV parks, Dextr has the more specific product.

## Proof and results

Both companies publish results, at different scales.

Dextr's results are measured property by property. Two properties of a 50-plus property hotel chain saw about $80,000 in extra revenue per property in the first two months, with 70% or more of calls handled start to finish by AI. A family-run RV park grew revenue 34% year on year, with 60% of its revenue credited to the voice agent. A campground saw 94% of 5,110 guest conversations resolved without staff. Property names are changed in these case studies.

Canary's AI Voice runs on a platform used by more than 20,000 hotels. Wyndham rolled it out to its franchisees after a pilot at more than 700 hotels. At Hotel Zephyr in San Francisco, AI Voice handled more than 800 guest calls in one April.

> **Key takeaway:** Dextr shows deeper results at individual properties. Canary shows reach and adoption by a major brand.

## Setup, support and pricing

With both products, your existing phone system stays in place. With Dextr, your number, phones and booking software stay as they are, and you set how and when the AI answers. Canary says AI Voice works with your existing phone system, with calls forwarded to a Canary number.

Dextr goes live in about 7 days per property: knowledge upload on day 2, booking software connected on day 3, testing on day 5, live on day 7. Groups go live 3 to 5 properties at a time. Each client gets a dedicated engineer and an AI strategist, and support is by email.

Canary says its AI tools go live in days, not weeks. Every bundle includes a dedicated onboarding and training lead, ongoing customer-success support and 24/7 support.

Neither company publishes prices. Dextr charges by use, based on your workflows, property size and volume, and offers a pilot on your toughest property that you can walk away from with no penalty. Canary prices its platform by custom quote; AI Voice is not part of its three listed bundles, so ask for a quote that includes it.

> **Key takeaway:** both set up in days and keep your phone system. Canary offers 24/7 support; Dextr offers a dedicated engineer and a walk-away pilot.

## What a missed call costs

Phone bookings still matter. Canary's own blog puts voice at about 18% of hotel reservations, and Revinate's data shows hotels convert about half (49.9%) of calls from people looking to book.

Many of those calls are never answered. Canary's AI Voice page says up to 40% of hotel calls go unanswered. In an AHLA survey published in March 2026, more than half of hoteliers said their properties were somewhat or severely understaffed.

Here is the maths for a hotel taking 60 booking calls a day, using a cautious 25% missed:

- 15 missed calls a day
- About half would have booked: 7 bookings
- At 2 nights and $150 a night: about $2,100 a day
- Over a 30-day month: about $63,000 in bookings at risk

A survey commissioned by Wyndham asked 325 hotel owners and developers across many brands about AI. The barriers they named most often were data privacy and security (46%), cost (42%) and connecting AI to older systems (40%). 57% said they need human oversight before AI makes operating decisions. Ask both vendors how they handle each of these.

> **Key takeaway:** whichever you choose, an AI that answers every call pays for itself quickly if your phone still books rooms.

## Questions to ask in any demo

1. Can the AI book, change and cancel a stay in my booking software while I watch?
2. How does the guest pay the deposit, and when?
3. When it isn't sure, what does it do, and what does my team receive?
4. How do I change a policy: a sentence, or a support ticket?
5. Who checks the calls, and what happens to the ones that go wrong?
6. What support do I get after go-live, and at what hours?

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the main difference between Dextr and Canary AI Voice?
Dextr builds AI agents around the phone call and hotel operations, and its voice agent sends the deposit payment link during the call. Canary AI Voice is the phone agent inside Canary's guest management platform, which also covers mobile check-in, mobile keys, guest messaging, upsells, tipping and payments.

### Which is better for a small independent hotel?
Both serve independent hotels. Dextr suits owners whose bookings come mostly by phone and who want deposits requested on the call. Canary suits owners who also want mobile check-in, keys and guest texting from the same vendor.

### Can either take a deposit on the phone?
Dextr sends the guest a payment link for the deposit before the call ends. Canary collects payment through its Payment Links and Digital Authorizations products, which staff or the system send by text or email.

### Do they work with my booking software?
Both connect to the major hotel systems, including Cloudbeds, Mews, Stayntouch and Oracle. Dextr also connects to dozens of vacation-rental systems such as Hostaway. Canary also connects to central reservation systems such as SynXis and Amadeus.

### Which has better support?
Canary includes 24/7 support, an onboarding lead and a customer-success manager. Dextr assigns a dedicated engineer and an AI strategist to each client, with support by email.

### Will guests know they are talking to an AI?
Daisy tells callers she is an AI if they ask, and never denies it. Canary's pages do not cover this, so ask in the demo.

### How quickly can each be live?
Dextr plans about 7 days per property. Canary says its AI tools go live in days, not weeks.

- **Check the handoff.** Ask to see exactly what your team receives when the AI passes a call over.
- **Compare the whole bill.** Ask what is included: phone agent, other products, support hours and setup.
- **Start small.** Pilot on one property before rolling out to the rest.
