# Best AI voice agents for hotels in 2026: 10 options compared

**Quick verdict:** the best AI voice agent for a hotel is one that finishes the job on the call: it checks live availability, books the stay into your PMS, takes the deposit and hands anything tricky to a person. Of the ten we compared, Dextr's Daisy is the strongest fit for independent and franchised hotels where the phone still books rooms, and for campgrounds, RV parks and rental portfolios. Canary AI Voice suits hotels already on Canary's guest platform, Conduit suits operators who want calls and texts on one agent with a published price, and PolyAI suits large groups and casino resorts.

**Disclosure:** this guide is published by Dextr, which makes Daisy, one of the voice agents below. We compared every product on the same six questions, using each company's own website and press releases, and we link to each one so you can check the facts yourself. Everything was checked on 14 October 2026.

> **Key takeaways**
> - Ask one question first: can the agent book a room during the call, or does it only take a message? Most hotel-built agents now book on the call.
> - Deposits are where they differ most. Daisy, Akia, PolyAI and EHVA say they take payment during the call, mostly by texting the guest a secure payment link.
> - Only one of the ten, Conduit, publishes its price. Everyone else, Dextr included, quotes after a demo.
> - Few hotel voice agents cover campgrounds and RV parks. Dextr has dedicated products for both.
> - Before you sign, make real test calls: a booking, a change, an after-hours question and a request for a person.

We asked the same six questions of every product, in the order a hotel buyer usually does:

## The 10 best AI voice agents for hotels

### 1. Dextr (Daisy)

**Best for:** independent and franchised hotels where the phone still books rooms, plus campgrounds, RV parks and vacation-rental portfolios.

[Daisy](https://www.dextr.ai/ai-reservations/) is Dextr's AI voice agent. It answers your main line 24/7, checks availability, quotes the rate, books, changes and cancels reservations, and texts the guest a payment link to secure the deposit during the call. It offers upsells such as room upgrades, early check-in and late checkout, and passes anything outside its knowledge base to your team with the caller's details attached.

- **Booking systems:** OPERA Cloud, Mews, Cloudbeds, Stayntouch, ResNexus, Hostaway, Guesty and Apaleo, plus more than 40 vacation-rental systems through one connection ([integrations](https://www.dextr.ai/integrations/)).
- **Setup:** live in about seven days. Your phone number, phones and PMS stay as they are.
- **Payments and security:** card numbers are tokenized by the payment processor and never stored by Dextr, and Dextr states it has completed a SOC 2 Type II audit ([responsible AI](https://www.dextr.ai/responsible-ai/)).
- **Keeping it current:** owners update policies, rates and discount codes in plain English, and every call is scored for quality.
- **Results:** at one family-run RV park, revenue grew 34% year on year and the voice agent [took more than $9,474 by phone across 39 bookings](https://www.dextr.ai/case-studies/elis-retreat-rv-park/). At two properties of a 50-plus property hotel chain, [the AI added about $80,000 in revenue per property in its first two months](https://www.dextr.ai/case-studies/reservation-contact-centers/). Each is one customer's result, with property names changed.
- **Pricing:** usage-based and not published; Dextr offers a pilot measured against the property's own trend.
- **Not the right fit if:** you want mobile keys and check-in kiosks from the same vendor, or you need a public price list before speaking to anyone.

It answers every call 24/7, answers guest questions, books and changes stays, promotes upsells and routes calls to the right team, while keeping your existing phone system and call-center software. Canary says its products are used by more than 20,000 hoteliers.

- **Payments:** the voice page doesn't say it takes payment during the call; Canary sells payment tools as separate products.
- **Worth knowing:** Canary says AI Voice won the 2026 HotelTechAwards category for voice and call-center software.
- **Pricing:** quote only.
- **Not the right fit if:** you only want a phone agent and none of the wider guest platform.

Since June 2026, Conduit says it can also take a booking in the PMS, change a reservation and reset a door code during the call.

- **Booking systems:** Opera Cloud, Mews, Cloudbeds, Apaleo, RMS Cloud and StayNTouch, plus rental systems such as Guesty and Hostaway.
- **Pricing:** a credit-based starting rate of $18 per unit a month that includes voice, or a Startup plan at $899 a month for up to 25 units ([pricing](https://www.conduit.ai/pricing)).
- **Payments:** Conduit doesn't say it takes payment during calls.
- **Languages:** detects the caller's language automatically; no count given.
- **Not the right fit if:** taking the deposit on the same call matters most to you.

## What an AI voice agent costs a hotel

Most hotel voice agents don't publish a price. Among the ten above, only Conduit does: a starting rate of $18 per unit a month that includes voice, or $899 a month for up to 25 units. PolyAI charges per minute without publishing the rate, and the build-it-yourself platforms charge $0.07 to $0.31 per minute before your own development time. Dextr's pricing is usage-based and depends on property size, workflows and call volume.

When you compare quotes, ask:

- Is it priced per room, per call, per minute or per property?
- What counts as a call, and are after-hours calls priced differently?
- Are setup, PMS connection and onboarding included?
- Can you start with one property, or with nights and weekends only?

> **Key takeaway:** compare quotes against the bookings you miss today, not just the monthly fee. An agent that books and takes the deposit on the call earns its cost back on the calls nobody answers now.

## Will guests know they're talking to an AI?

They often ask, and the safest answer is yes. Set the agent to say it's an AI when asked, and make it easy to reach a person at any point. Dextr's agents say they're AI when asked and always leave a route to your team. Rules on AI disclosure and call recording vary by state and province, so check yours before you go live.

## When an AI voice agent isn't the right choice

- **Most of your calls are complex group or event sales.** An agent can qualify them, but a person should close.
- **Your PMS has no connection to the agent.** Without one, it can only take messages, which is what voicemail already does.
- **Nobody can review calls in the first few weeks.** Every agent needs its answers checked and corrected early on.
- **Your brand promises a person on every call.** Use AI for after-hours and overflow only.

## Questions to ask in any demo

1. Can you book a room on this call, into my PMS, while I watch?
2. Can you take a deposit during the call, and how is the card handled?
3. What happens when I ask for a person, and what does my team see?
4. If I call after hours with a question you can't answer, what happens next?
5. Which languages do you support, and can I test one of mine now?
6. Can I change a policy myself, and how soon does the agent use it?
7. What exactly will I pay each month, and what counts as a call?
8. Can I hear recorded calls from a property like mine?

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best AI voice agent for hotels?

It depends on your property. For independent and franchised hotels where the phone books rooms, Dextr's Daisy books the stay, takes the deposit and hands off to staff on the same call. Canary AI Voice suits hotels already on Canary, Conduit suits operators who want a published price, and PolyAI suits large groups and casino resorts.

### Can an AI voice agent book a hotel room over the phone?

Yes, if it connects to your PMS and is allowed to create reservations. Most of the ten agents in this guide say they book during the call. Ask to see a live booking land in your own PMS during the demo.

### How much does an AI voice agent cost for a hotel?

Most vendors quote after a demo. Conduit publishes a starting rate of $18 per unit a month that includes voice, and build-it-yourself platforms charge $0.07 to $0.31 per minute. Dextr's pricing is usage-based and depends on property size, workflows and call volume.

### Will an AI voice agent replace my front desk?

No. It answers routine calls such as availability, rates, bookings and directions, so your team can focus on guests in front of them. Anything outside its knowledge goes to a person with the details already captured.

### Does an AI voice agent work with my PMS?

Check the vendor's named list. Dextr names OPERA Cloud, Mews, Cloudbeds, Stayntouch, ResNexus, Hostaway, Guesty and Apaleo. Also ask whether the agent only reads your PMS or can write bookings into it.

### Can an AI voice agent take card payments safely?

The common approach is to text the guest a secure payment link during the call, so the card number is never read aloud. With Dextr, card numbers are tokenized by the payment processor and never stored by Dextr. Ask every vendor how cards are handled and which PCI standard applies.

### How long does it take to set up an AI voice agent?

Dextr goes live in about seven days, and your phone number, phones and PMS stay as they are. Other vendors range from days to weeks depending on the PMS connection, so ask for a dated plan.

### Do AI voice agents speak Spanish and other languages?

Most handle several languages, but the counts vary and some vendors give more than one figure. Test the languages your guests actually use during the demo.
