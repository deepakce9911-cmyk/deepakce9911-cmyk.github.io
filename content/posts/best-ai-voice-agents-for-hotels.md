# Best AI voice agents for hotels in 2026: 10 options compared

**Quick verdict:** the best AI voice agent for a hotel is one that finishes the job on the call: it checks live availability, books the stay into your PMS, takes the deposit and hands anything tricky to a person. Of the ten we compared, Dextr's Daisy is the strongest fit for independent and franchised hotels where the phone still books rooms, and for campgrounds, RV parks and rental portfolios. Canary AI Voice suits hotels already on Canary's guest platform, Conduit suits operators who want calls and texts on one agent with a published price, and PolyAI suits large groups and casino resorts.

**Disclosure:** this guide is published by Dextr, which makes Daisy, one of the voice agents below. We compared every product on the same six questions, using each company's own website and press releases, and we link to those pages so you can check the facts yourself. Everything was checked on 14 October 2026.

> **Key takeaways**
> - Ask one question first: can the agent book a room during the call, or does it only take a message? Most hotel-built agents now book on the call.
> - Deposits are where they differ most. Daisy, Akia, PolyAI and EHVA say they take payment during the call. Others mention payments or deposits without saying how they work on a call.
> - Three of the ten show a price up front: Conduit, Sadie and EHVA. Everyone else, Dextr included, quotes on request.
> - Few hotel voice agents talk about campgrounds and RV parks. Dextr markets its agents as purpose-built for both.
> - Before you sign, make real test calls: a booking, a change, an after-hours question and a request for a person.

## What an AI voice agent costs a hotel

Most hotel voice agents don't publish a price. Among the ten above, three do:

- **Conduit:** $899 a month for up to 25 units, and from $18 per unit a month, voice included, for larger portfolios.
- **Sadie:** hotel plans at $199, $279 and $499 a month for up to 300, 600 and 1,200 minutes.
- **EHVA:** about 9 cents a minute with no contract, according to its homepage.

PolyAI charges per minute without giving the rate on its pricing page. Retell and Bland list $0.07 to $0.31 per minute plus phone costs, and Synthflow's contracts start at $30,000 a year. Dextr's pricing is usage-based and depends on property size, workflows and usage volume.

When you compare quotes, ask:

- Is it priced per room, per call, per minute or per property?
- What counts as a call, and are after-hours calls priced differently?
- Are setup, PMS connection and onboarding included?
- Can you start with one property, or with nights and weekends only?

> **Key takeaway:** compare quotes against the bookings you miss today, not just the monthly fee. An agent that books and takes the deposit on the call earns its cost back on the calls nobody answers now.

## Will guests know they're talking to an AI?

They often ask, and the safest answer is yes. Set the agent to say it's an AI when asked, and make it easy to reach a person at any point. Dextr's agents say they're AI when asked and always leave a route to your team. Rules on AI disclosure and call recording vary by state and province, so check yours before you go live.

## When an AI voice agent isn't the right choice

- **Most of your calls are complex group or event sales.** An agent can qualify them, but a person should close.
- **Your PMS has no connection to the agent.** Without one, it can only take messages, which is what voicemail already does.
- **Nobody can review calls in the first few weeks.** Every agent needs its answers checked and corrected early on.
- **Your brand promises a person on every call.** Use AI for after-hours and overflow only.

## Questions to ask in any demo

1. Can you book a room on this call, into my PMS, while I watch?
2. Can you take a deposit during the call, and how is the card handled?
3. What happens when I ask for a person, and what does my team see?
4. If I call after hours with a question you can't answer, what happens next?
5. Which languages do you support, and can I test one of mine now?
6. Can I change a policy myself, and how soon does the agent use it?
7. What exactly will I pay each month, and what counts as a call?
8. Can I hear recorded calls from a property like mine?
